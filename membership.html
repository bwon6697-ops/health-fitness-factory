COMMON QUESTIONS

How do I join? +
Simply visit our gym at Mehnagar or click "Choose Plan" to sign up online. Fill in your details and become a member instantly.
What are the payment methods? +
We accept Cash, UPI, and Bank Transfers. You can pay directly at the gym reception.
Can I get a trial session? +
Yes! We offer a one-day trial session for newcomers. Contact us to schedule yours.
Is there any joining fee? +
No. We have zero hidden joining fees. You only pay for the membership duration you choose.
What should I bring? +
Please bring comfortable workout clothes, sports shoes, a water bottle, and a small towel.
Can I cancel my membership? +
Yes, you can stop anytime. However, please note that membership fees are non-refundable.
Do you have personal trainers? +
Yes, we have certified trainers on floor at all times. Specialized 1-on-1 personal training is also available.
What is the age limit? +
We welcome everyone aged 14 and above. Members under 18 require parental consent.
